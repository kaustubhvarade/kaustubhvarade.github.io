## The starting point

Opening a cash ISA meant a branch visit or a paper form, and checks that ran days after the customer had left.

## The constraints

Every account still had to pass identity verification, anti-money-laundering screening and FATCA tax checks. The core banking platform also expected each account in a fixed format.

## What we built

A digital journey that ran the checks while the customer was still in the flow, and created the account in the core system as the last step rather than the first.

## The outcome

For most customers, opening an account went from days to minutes. Exceptions went to a person instead of holding everyone up.

## What I'd do differently

Involve the operations team that handles exceptions from day one. The happy path was the easy part.
